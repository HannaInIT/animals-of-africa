# Cheetah

The cheetah (Acinonyx jubatus) is a large *cat* and the *fastest* land animal. It has a tawny to creamy white or pale buff fur that is marked with evenly spaced, solid black spots. The head is small and rounded, with a short *snout* and black tear-like facial streaks. It reaches 67–94 cm at the shoulder, and the head-and-body length is between 1.1 and 1.5 m. Adults weigh between 21 and 65 kg. The cheetah is capable of running at 93 to 104 km/h; it has evolved specialized adaptations for speed, including a light build, long thin legs and a long tail. 

It is now distributed mainly in small, fragmented populations in northwestern, eastern and southern Africa and central Iran. It lives in a variety of habitats such as savannahs in the Serengeti, arid mountain ranges in the Sahara, and hilly desert terrain.
